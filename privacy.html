Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-12

1. What we collect

Nothing. Pennora is a set of calculators that run entirely in your web browser. The numbers you type — balances, incomes, debts, goals — are processed on your device and are never sent to our servers.

2. Cookies, web beacons, and analytics

Pennora itself does not set tracking cookies or collect personal data. If third-party services such as Google AdSense are enabled, those services may use standard cookies, web beacons (also called pixel tags), and may collect technical information such as your IP address, subject to their own privacy policies. You can control cookies through your browser settings. For details on how Google uses data, see How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

3. Affiliate links

Some links (for example, to books) are affiliate links. If you click and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. These links are marked as sponsored. Affiliate partners may set their own cookies under their policies.

4. Children's privacy

Pennora is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

5. Your choices

You can clear stored preferences (language and theme) by clearing this site's data in your browser. Because we store no personal information, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@pennora.com.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026-07-12

1. 我们收集什么

什么都不收集。 Pennora 是一组完全在你的网页浏览器中运行的计算器。你输入的数字——余额、收入、债务、目标——都在你的设备上处理，绝不会发送到我们的服务器。

2. Cookie、网络信标与分析

Pennora 本身不设置跟踪 Cookie，也不收集个人数据。如果启用了 Google AdSense 等第三方服务，这些服务可能会根据它们各自的隐私政策使用标准 Cookie、网络信标（也称像素标签），并可能收集你的 IP 地址等技术信息。你可以通过浏览器的设置来控制 Cookie。关于 Google 如何使用数据，请参阅 Google 对合作方站点与应用的数据使用说明。

3. 联盟链接

部分链接（例如书籍链接）是联盟链接。如果你点击并完成了购买，我们可能会获得一笔佣金，而不会增加你的任何费用。这些链接已标记为「赞助」。联盟合作伙伴可能会根据它们的政策设置自己的 Cookie。

4. 儿童隐私

Pennora 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会在知情的情况下收集他们的信息。

5. 你的选择

你可以通过在浏览器中清除本站的数据，来清除已保存的偏好设置（语言和主题）。由于我们不存储任何个人信息，我们这边没有需要删除的内容。

6. 联系我们

如有隐私相关问题，请联系 privacy@pennora.com。
