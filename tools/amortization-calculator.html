What it does

Build a full amortization schedule for a fixed-rate loan, with optional extra monthly payments that cut interest and shorten the term.

How it works

Monthly payment M = P·r / (1 − (1+r)^−n), where P is principal, r the monthly rate, n the number of payments. Each period: interest = balance·r; principal = payment − interest.

Example

$350,000 at 6.5% over 30 years
about $2,212.24/month; ~$446k total interest.

Questions people ask

What does an extra payment do?
It goes straight to principal, reducing future interest and paying the loan off earlier.
Why does my total interest differ slightly from other sites?
Small differences come from rounding the monthly payment. The formula is standard.
