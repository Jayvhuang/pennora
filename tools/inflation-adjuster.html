Inflation Adjuster

Convert any past or future amount into today’s real value.

Open the free calculator →

What it does

See what a sum of money is worth after accounting for inflation over a number of years.

How it works

Real value = Nominal ÷ (1+inflation)^years.

Example

$1,000,000 in 30 years at 3% inflation
about $411,987 in today’s dollars.

Questions people ask

What inflation rate should I use?
Long-run U.S. averages are ~2–3%. Use 3% for a conservative real-value estimate.
Does this account for investment growth?
No — it only deflates nominal money. Pair it with the compound calculator to model growth and inflation together.
