What it does

Calculate a 3–6 (or 6–9) month expense buffer based on your essential monthly spending.

How it works

Fund = essential monthly expenses × months of coverage (commonly 3–6; 6–9 if income is unstable or you have dependents).

Example

$3,000 essential monthly spend, 6 months
$18,000 emergency fund.

Questions people ask

Why 3–6 months?
It covers most job-loss or surprise-expense gaps without tying up too much cash.
Where should I keep it?
In a liquid, low-risk account (high-yield savings), not invested.
