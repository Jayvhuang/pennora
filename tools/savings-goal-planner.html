What it does

Enter a goal amount, timeframe, and expected return, and see the monthly contribution needed to get there.

How it works

Monthly contribution = Goal ÷ [((1+r)^n − 1)/r], where r is the monthly rate and n the number of months.

Example

Save $50,000 in 5 years at 5%
about $736 per month.

Questions people ask

Should I include investment returns?
Only if the money will be invested. For a cash goal (e.g. a house deposit), set the rate to 0 for a conservative plan.
What if I can’t hit the monthly number?
Lengthen the timeline, lower the goal, or raise expected return — the planner shows the trade-off instantly.
