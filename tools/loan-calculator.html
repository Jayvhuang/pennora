What it does

Enter vehicle price, down payment, trade-in, APR, and term to see the financed amount, monthly payment, and total interest.

How it works

Financed = price − down payment − trade-in. Monthly payment uses the standard amortization formula M = P·r / (1 − (1+r)^−n).

Example

$32,000 car, $5,000 down, 7.5% APR, 60 months
about $541/month.

Questions people ask

What term should I pick?
Longer terms lower the monthly payment but cost more interest overall.
Does trade-in reduce my payment?
Yes — it lowers the amount you finance, just like a bigger down payment.
