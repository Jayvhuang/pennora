What it does

Estimate take-home pay from gross wages, pre-tax deductions (401(k), health), federal and state income tax, and FICA.

How it works

Net = Gross − pre-tax deductions − federal tax − state tax − FICA (Social Security 6.2% + Medicare 1.45%, plus 0.9% Additional Medicare above $200k).

Example

$75,000 salary, single, biweekly
roughly $2,343 net per pay period.

Questions people ask

Why is my take-home less than gross?
Taxes and pre-tax deductions come out first. FICA alone is 7.65% of most wages.
Is this exact?
It is a U.S. 2024 estimate using standard brackets and a flat state rate you enter. Your payroll may differ slightly.
