What it does

Compute federal income tax using 2024 brackets across four filing statuses, standard deduction, FICA, and an optional flat state rate.

How it works

Taxable income = AGI − standard deduction. Federal tax = sum of bracket rates applied to each portion. FICA adds 7.65% (employer+employee) up to the Social Security cap, plus 0.9% Additional Medicare above $200k.

Example

$75,000 single
about $8,341 federal, ~11.1% effective, 22% marginal.

Questions people ask

What is marginal vs effective rate?
Marginal is the rate on your last dollar; effective is total tax ÷ total income. Effective is always lower.
Do you handle itemized deductions?
No — this uses the standard deduction for simplicity. Itemizing changes the result only if your deductions exceed it.
